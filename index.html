2-Minute Setup on your iPhone:

1
Install the free Scriptable app

Download Scriptable from the App Store (100% free, no ads).

2
Copy the Widget Code

Click below to copy your pre-configured widget script. It comes already set up with your synced words URL!

Copied to Clipboard! ✓
3
Paste in Scriptable & Add to Home Screen
  1. Open Scriptable on your iPhone and tap + (new script).
  2. Paste the copied script and name it "Lexicon". Tap Done.
  3. Go to your iPhone Home Screen, long-press any empty area, tap + in the top corner.
  4. Search for Scriptable, choose Medium or Small widget, and tap Add Widget.
  5. Long-press the new widget -> Edit Widget -> Set Script to "Lexicon". Done! 🎉
